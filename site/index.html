    About this portfolio

    These are exercises, not client engagements: each one starts from a realistic scenario, states its constraints, and requires a decision that can be defended under challenge. Scenarios and organisations are fictional. The measurements, framework references and failure modes are the ones that appear in real audits.

    Every project follows the same test: would this survive an auditor who assumes you built it to pass? Where the answer is no, the limitation is written down rather than hidden.

    Active Directory projects cover on-premises and hybrid identity only. Cloud-only privilege (Entra roles, service principals, workload identities) is an acknowledged gap, listed in the bridge document and a candidate for Project 09.
