# The 30-Day Plan: Day 1 Is Today

**Start:** Friday 30 October 2026 · **Applications begin:** Day 7 (Thursday 5 November) — not Day 30

The plan assumes 2–3 focused hours per day alongside work or study. If you have fewer hours, cut the *ticket count* and the *application count* — never the write-ups. Twelve documented tickets beat sixteen undocumented ones.

---

## The four rules

1. **Apply from Day 7.** A plan that ends with "now start applying" is a 60-day plan.
2. **Fix findings 1, 2 and 4 before anything else.** Over-privilege, no separate admin account, no recycle bin. Ninety minutes total, and they change your interview.
3. **Every ticket ends with a note you would be happy for an interviewer to read.**
4. **Miss a day, do not restart.** Move the task to tomorrow and keep the week's theme.
